Tube Player __VERSION__

YouTube tutorials in a resizable plugin window, inside Ableton Live.

  • Installs the VST3 and Audio Unit versions for every user on this Mac.
  • Runs natively on Apple Silicon and on Intel Macs (macOS 11 or newer).
  • Replaces any earlier copy of Tube Player, including one dragged into your plug-in folders by hand.
