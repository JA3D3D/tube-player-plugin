Switch on the Live helper

The helper is the part of AbletonAi that lives inside Live and does the building. The installer already put it in your User Library.

  1. If Live was open while you installed, quit Live and open it again.
  2. Open Live’s Settings (Cmd + ,) and go to Link, Tempo & MIDI.
  3. In an empty Control Surface slot, choose AbletonAi. Leave Input and Output on None.
  4. The dot at the top of AbletonAi turns green.

Not in the list? Your User Library may be somewhere else (Live Settings › Library). Copy the AbletonAi folder from /Library/Application Support/AbletonAi/Remote Scripts into “User Library/Remote Scripts” there and restart Live.
