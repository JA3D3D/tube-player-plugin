AbletonAi __VERSION__

An AI producer inside Ableton Live: it writes MIDI, builds tracks with Live’s instruments and effects, and sketches parts or whole songs in your set.

  • The VST3 and Audio Unit plug-ins, for every user on this Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer).
  • The Live helper, copied into your Live User Library, so AbletonAi can build in your set.

You’ll need a Claude API key from console.anthropic.com (use is billed to your Anthropic account). Quit Live before you continue.
